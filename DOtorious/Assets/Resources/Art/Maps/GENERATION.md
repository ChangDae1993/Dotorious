## SeasonalTerrainAtlas.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production texture atlas for a Unity 2D side-scrolling pixel-art map construction kit. Primary request: a precisely aligned FIVE COLUMNS by TWO ROWS atlas, ten SQUARE cells of side-view ground cross-section textures, filling the whole canvas in a landscape 5:2 aspect ratio. Cells share identical geometry: straight perfectly horizontal walkable surface at the TOP edge, thin natural surface cap occupying top 12%, solid detailed earth/rock beneath down to bottom; full rectangular texture, NOT floating islands, no sky. Crisp hand-crafted dark-fantasy pixel art, visible square pixels, limited carefully harmonized palettes, subtle readable details, orthographic SIDE VIEW, no 3D perspective, no labels or borders or padding. Each cell repeats seamlessly left-right so adjacent pieces join. Reading order left to right top then bottom: v01 spring meadow green grass/dark loam, v02 spring blossom grove pink fallen petals/green moss brown earth, v03 summer deep forest rich emerald turf/root-rich umber soil, v04 summer coast golden sand/sandstone small shells, v05 autumn amber woodland ochre fallen leaves/dark soil roots, v06 autumn crimson grove red fallen leaves/muted grey-brown rock, v07 winter snowy forest white snow/cold charcoal rocky dirt/icicles, v08 winter frozen lake pale cyan ice/deep blue crystal stone, v09 spring mossy ruins bright moss/weathered grey stone blocks, v10 winter ruined fortress thick snow/blue-grey old masonry. All ten cells visually distinct and richly textured but belong to one consistent game art style. Important: each rectangle completely filled, zero text, zero grid lines, no characters, no shadows outside the texture. Matching versions will be used on floors, slopes, stairs, platforms, bridges and walls.

## SeasonalSceneryAtlas.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG sprite atlas for a 2D side-scrolling pixel-art map kit, matching ten seasonal terrain versions. Primary request: FIVE COLUMNS by TWO ROWS, ten equally sized square cells in one 5:2 landscape canvas. Each cell contains TWO SEPARATE isolated cutout sprites: a tall tree/group occupying the LEFT 70 percent, and a small matching rock, shrub and ground decoration group occupying the RIGHT 25 percent near the bottom. Leave transparent clearance between cells and between the two sprites, nothing touches cell borders. Keep base level at 94 percent down within each cell. Entire background genuinely transparent alpha, no ground plane, no environment rectangles, no fake checkerboard. Crisp square-pixel handcrafted dark-fantasy game art, orthographic side view, carefully clustered pixels, same stylistic family across seasons, no gradients/blur, no character/UI/text/grid/borders. Reading order row-major: v01 SpringMeadow lush green oak with delicate white blossoms and green moss rocks; v02 SpringBlossom pink flowering cherry tree, pink flowers and brown moss rock; v03 SummerForest dense emerald oak and ferns around rooty umber rocks; v04 SummerCoast windswept coastal pine, pale sandstone and golden grass shells; v05 AutumnAmber golden ochre oak, orange leaf pile and dark brown rocks; v06 AutumnCrimson red maple, red shrubs and muted grey stone; v07 WinterSnow snowy bare forest tree, white snowbank and dark charcoal rocks; v08 WinterIce frost-encrusted pine, pale cyan icicles and blue ice crystal rocks; v09 SpringMossRuins ancient green willow beside small mossy broken grey stone blocks, ferns and a ruined plinth; v10 WinterStoneRuins snow-laden old bare tree beside dark blue-grey ruined pillar, snow-covered masonry and dead twigs.
